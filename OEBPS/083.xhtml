P ersonality style is defined as the way people act when

mean that people act the same way all the time, but behavior
 is consistent to the point that it’s predictable. This chapter
 contains the top ten Personality Style tips that help you make

No One Personality Style
 Is Better Than Another
Which personality style is best? That question may be one of
 the most frequently asked ones. The answer? In short, they all
 are. Each style has its own unique characteristics that in the
 right circumstance and at the right time come to the forefront.
