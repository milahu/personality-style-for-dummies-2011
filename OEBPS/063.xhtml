Considerate Style
The Considerate Style works best when coachees want an
 empathetic, supportive, and encouraging coach. Coachees
 who need a friendly relationship work well with this style.
The Benefits of the Considerate Style in coaching include
vr Encourages self-learning and personal insight
Vv” Listens actively and provides support during all stages of
 the coaching process
vw Creates a comfortable, encouraging environment
The potential trouble behaviors to look out for with the
 Considerate Style are
vr Overly focusing on rapport, which can lead them to
 expect less from the coachee
vr Lacking directness, which may lead to a lack of clarity
 about key issues or challenges
vr Rather than approaching some issues directly and with
 initiative, spending too much time trying to facilitate per-
 sonal insight
This style doesn’t work well when coachees want a more facil-
 itative, results-oriented approach. This empathic style may
 feel invasive to some coachees.
