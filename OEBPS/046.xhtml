Benefits and Challenges
 of the Four Personality
 Styles in Selling
Building a successful sales relationship can be challenging. The
 buyer wants to trust the seller and believe that the seller is
 looking out for everyone’s best interest in the relationship. The
 comfort that a buyer has with a seller depends on both of their
 personality styles. In this section, you discover how the four
 personality styles benefit and challenge a sales relationship.
