vr Considerate Time Management Style can result in so
 much time being spent helping others that you take on
 too much and don’t complete your own tasks. Keeping
 your own needs in the forefront benefits those with this
 style.
Tactics to use to enhance your awareness of when to flex
 include:
= v Evaluate how much time you have to complete a task or
 project. Are deadlines flexible? Are others waiting for
 you?
vr Evaluate tasks. Is the big picture enough or do I need to
 really focus on the small details?
vr Consider whether engaging others to explore alternative
 processes may enhance the outcome.
w Prioritize current tasks. Is this urgent? Are others
 depending on me to finish this now?
Of course the most effective time management results from
 doing things correctly and meeting deadlines; however, it isn’t
 always easy. Each task has different priorities, deadlines, and
 associated skills. In some instances the deadline must be met
 at all costs. In other instances the smallest details are vital
to success. Interpreting each situation and being able to flex
 one’s style is essential.
