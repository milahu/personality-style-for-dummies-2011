Making the Direct Style flex
Flexing the Direct Style has two avenues: reducing the degree
 of assertiveness and increasing emotional expressions of
 warmth and regard. The Direct Style is useful in situations
 when you need to:
wv Get to the bottom line quickly
vr Speak forcefully (as in preventing an accident)
. V Assert control to communicate authority and direction
Those with the Direct Style need to calibrate how much asser-
 tive behavior is appropriate and how much emotional energy
 to show in certain situations. For example, demonstrating a
 Direct Style while dealing with an immediate crisis works very
 well, but it may not be as effective when dealing with change
 when people’s emotional needs may need to be considered.
