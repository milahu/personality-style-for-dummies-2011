Style Is Seen in Words, Tone of
 Voice, and Nonverbal Actions
It’s not just the words you use. People read into the sounds
 you make, whether harsh, pleasant, rapid, sarcastic, or what-
 ever. People read into your nonverbal actions — whether
 they're attentive, bored, or angry. Your expression and stance
 may show how you truly feel. The way you stand, shake
 hands, make eye contact, or not, all say just as much as the
 words you choose.
Make your words, tone of voice, and actions consistent in
 order to not confuse your message.
