One of the most important determinants of learning effec-
 tiveness is the fit of learning style and with the tactic that is
 needed for the learning situation. If a Considerate Learning
 Style attempts to use a Direct Learning Style tactic and it
 doesn’t work with that learning situation, then the learning
 may be short circuited.
Flexing requires you to assess the needs of the interaction
 and to shift (even momentarily) in your choice of a response.
 You need to determine how much control and direction
 (assertiveness) and how much emotional connection (expres-
 siveness) is required of you in a learning situation.
