Of course, the most important determinant of the sales pro-
 cess is the fit of seller style and seller knowledge with the
 needs and style of the buyer. If a Considerate Style seller
 attempts to work with a Direct Style buyer and neither knows
 how to flex, then the selling experience is likely to be short
 lived.
Flexing requires you to assess the needs of the interaction
 and to shift (even momentarily) in your choice of a response.
 You need to determine how much assertiveness and emo-
 tional expressiveness is required of you in an interaction.
Some of the common flexing challenges for the four selling
 styles are as follows:
= vw” Direct Style sellers can fail to recognize that a buyer was
 offended by a direct comment or that a buyer wants to
 move at a different pace. Knowing when to increase some
 emotional expressiveness and tone down assertiveness
 benefits those with this selling style.
vw Spirited Style sellers can fail to recognize when the
 buyer wants a concrete plan and wants to know what the
 details are related to the product or service. Knowing
 when to moderate both assertiveness (around informa-
 tion) and emotional expressiveness benefits those with
 this selling style.
vr Systematic Style sellers can fail to recognize that a buyer
 wants more brainstorming of possibilities and greater
 enthusiasm about the product or service. Knowing when
 to elevate both assertiveness and emotional expressive-
 ness helps those with this selling style.
vr Considerate Style sellers can fail to recognize that a buyer

 on warm and empathetic interactions. Knowing when to
 elevate an assertive response and decrease emotional
