Systematic Style
This style works best when the team members want a detailed
 plan that’s data-focused and pragmatic. Team members who
 work best with this style want an objective, methodical team
 member strategy. This style says, “With careful planning and
 organization of priorities, we can meet our deadline and get
 on to the next issue to solve.”
Those who favor this style provide the following benefits:
vr Thorough attention to process, which builds confidence
 in the team plan
vr Focuses on facts, not feelings, which generates a clear
 understanding of what needs to be done
vr Keeps the team focused on the priorities of the moment
