Spirited Style
The Spirited Style succeeds when followers enjoy enthusiastic
 motivation and a less structured approach. Other pluses of
 this style in leading include the following:
w This leader motivates others to be creative and generate
 fresh ideas.
w This leader is spontaneously expressive, warm, and
 supportive.
vw This leader sees the big picture, context, and overall
 vision.
This leader may get into trouble in the following areas:
 vr Energy and enthusiasm may overwhelm others, prevent-
 ing them from sharing their perspectives
vw Over eagerness can lead to glossing over of important
 details
vr Seen as impulsive and disorganized prompting followers
 to feel unproductive
The Spirited Style leader doesn’t work well when followers
 need a planned approach to tasks, roles, and goals.
