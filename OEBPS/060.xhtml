As you engage in each of these elements of coaching, the
 use of your personality style reveals your coaching style.
 Importantly, learning to flex your personality style enhances
 your coaching effectiveness as you engage with coachees
 through each element of coaching. Your coaching behavior
 and your personality style make up your coaching style.
All four personality styles can be equally effective in coaching.
 However, styles that don’t match the needs of the coachee
 can result in a failed coaching relationship. You need to
 understand the benefits and potential challenges each style

Looking at the Benefits and
 Trouble Spots in Coaching
Your coaching style has the potential to work really well
 with some coachees and not at all with others. The first step
 in becoming a successful coach is understanding your style
 and how it can be most effective in coaching situations. This
 section helps you understand your own coaching behavioral
 strengths and potential trouble spots and why your coaching
 methods only work some of the time.
