Engaging with a Systematic
 Style Person
When we say “just the facts, please,” we mean “just the facts,
 please.” A Systematic Style person doesn’t have time for idle
 chitchat or speculation; however, these detail-oriented people
 are an asset to any project or team.
To make the most of your interactions with the Systematic
 Style person, try the following tips:
1. Focus on facts, not opinions.
 2. Be thorough and organized.
 3. Avoid gimmicks.
4. Allow time for analysis.
5. Provide written evidence when possible.
