Systematic Style
When the audience or recipient of the message wants a
 detailed and thoroughly factual message is when this style
 hits a home run. Audiences who respond best with this style
 want an objective, methodical communication strategy.
 Individuals with this style tend to provide precise statements,
 brief comments with little emotional expression, little anima-
 tion, and keep physical distance.
Folks who favor this style display the following benefits in
 communication:
vr Thorough attention to process and details in giving the
 message and in the message itself
vr Factual and pragmatic, concise and precise messages
. v Careful, patient, reserved in delivering information
