Flexing the Four Personality
 Styles in Leading
To increase overall effectiveness, leaders need to learn to flex
 their style as the situation requires. For example, the follow-
 ing elements of leading may be more efficiently achieved with
 different styles:
vw Establishing a relationship is efficiently achieved with the
 Considerate Style.
vw Creating a common understanding of the issue and the
 strategy for addressing the desired goals easily flows
 from the Systematic Style.
vr Supporting the implementation of the plan comes
 naturally to the Spirited Style.
vr Monitoring progress and re-evaluating future courses of
 action are effectively managed with the Direct Style.
Of course, the most important determinant of leader success
 is the fit of leader style and knowledge with the needs of the
 followers. If a Considerate Style leader attempts to work with
 a Direct Style follower and neither knows how to flex, then the
 leader-follower relationship is likely to be short lived.
