 individual tasks but also with one-on-one and group inter-
 actions. How you manage your time varies from person to
 person, and these differences show up in everything you do.
 To fully understand time style, it’s important to learn how it
 drives behaviors and affects daily interactions and choices for
 spending time on tasks.
Your level of assertiveness and expressiveness affects how
 you manage time, resulting in your time management style.
 More assertive people tend to channel their energy into
 completing tasks quickly, while less assertive people prefer
 a slower pace where all details are fully examined and every-
 one’s needs are accounted for. Less expressive people prefer
 less personal contact and more analysis time, while more
 expressive people prefer spontaneity over planning.
