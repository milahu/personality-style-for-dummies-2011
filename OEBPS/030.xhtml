As you engage in each of these elements of leading, the use of
 your personality style reveals your leading style. Importantly,
 learning to flex your personality style enhances your leader
 effectiveness as you engage with others through each element
 of leading. Your leader behavior and your personality style
 make up your leader style.
All leading styles can be equally effective. Although, leading
 styles that don’t match the needs of the followers can result
 in a failed leadership initiative. You need to understand
the benefits and potential challenges each style tends to
 demonstrate.
Checking Out the Benefits and
 Trouble Spots of Each Style
Leading others means behaving in ways that inspire and
 motivate people. Each of the four personality styles brings
 specific benefits to leaders. When your style “fits” the leader-
 ship demands of the situation and the people you're leading,
 the benefits are numerous. Also, by knowing the trouble spots
 of the style as they pertain to leading, you can become more
 aware of what behaviors to avoid when leading others in any
 situation. In this section, you find out how best to use your
 style to lead others.
Direct Style
The person with this style works best when followers need
 decisive direction and responds well to a fast-moving, aggres-
 sive leadership approach.
