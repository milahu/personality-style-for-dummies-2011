How This Book Is Organized
The 12 chapters of this book move from an introduction of
 the four personality styles to the application of those styles
 in various work situations. You even get a few chapters at the
 end with some tips and tricks of personality style.
You're encouraged to read the first two chapters in order, but
 like any For Dummies book, you don’t have to. Try visiting the
 remaining chapters as the topic becomes important to you.
 You can jump in wherever best suits your knowledge needs —
 check out the Table of Contents at the beginning of this book
 to see where you want to go. Or, as with most books, you can
 start at the beginning and read all the way to the end, if you
 want.
