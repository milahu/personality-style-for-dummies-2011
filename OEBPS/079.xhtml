Taking on the Direct Style
Feeling intimidated by the strong, forceful Direct Style person-
 ality? They aren’t as tough as they seem — just very focused.
To help your interactions go smoothly, keep in mind these
 tips when dealing with the Direct Style:
1. Focus on the person’s goals and objectives.
 2. Keep your relationship businesslike.
3. Be well organized.
4. Speak at a fast pace.
5. Ask direct questions.
