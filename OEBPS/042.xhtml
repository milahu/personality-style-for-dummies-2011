Flexing the Four Personality
 Styles in Teaming
To increase overall effectiveness, team members need to
 learn to flex their style as the situation requires. For example,
 the following elements of teaming may be more efficiently
 achieved with different styles:
~~ Establishing a connection among team members so
everyone is clear as to why they're members of the team
 7 or involved at a particular time in the process is natural
 to the Considerate Team Member Style.
vr Creating a common understanding of the goals, methods,
 and plans for the team to do its work comes naturally to
 the Systematic Team Member Style.
=” Supporting each other in fulfilling the mutually agreed on
 plan is easily energized by the Spirited Team Member
 Style.
\ v“ Monitoring progress and re-evaluating future courses of
 . action comes easily to the Direct Team Member Style.
Of course, an important determinant of the success of team-
 ing processes is how well each style’s benefits are used by
 the team and how well each team member flexes to work with
 others who are different. If a Considerate Team Member Style
 attempts to work with a Direct Team Member Style and nei-
 ther knows how to flex, then the teaming efforts are likely to
 be short lived.
Flexing requires you to assess the needs of the interaction
 and to shift (even momentarily) in your choice of a response.
 You need to determine how much assertiveness and emo-
 tional expressiveness is required of you in an interaction.
