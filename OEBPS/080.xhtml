Keeping Up with the
Spirited Style
 Have a co-worker who's full of energy and great ideas but
 hard to work with because, well, she’s full of energy and great
ideas?
Try some of the following tips:
1. Be entertaining.
2. Engage in brainstorming.
3. Allow her to share her ideas freely.
 4. Keep things moving.
5. Focus on inspiring ideas.
Cooperating with the
 Considerate Style
No one can be that nice — he must have ulterior motives,
 right? Nope, that’s just his Considerate Personality Style
 coming through. He really does want to help and support you,
 even at the expense of his own work. Go with it, but remem-
 ber to return the favor.
To help you get the most out of your interactions, here are
 some tips:
1. Focus on your relationship.
2. Be supportive of his feelings.
3. Make sure you understand his needs.
 4. Maintain a relaxed pace.
5. Give him time to build trust in you.
