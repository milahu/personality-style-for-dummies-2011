After you've decided the message you want to give to an indi-
 vidual or to a group, you need to attend to the basic elements
 of communicating a message:
vw Establishing a connection with the audience of one or
 one thousand, making sure that the reason for the com-
 munication is clear
vw Creating a common understanding of the message being
 delivered and received
vw Facilitating the exploration of the implications of the
 communication
vr Wrapping up the message and verifying common
 understanding
As you engage in each of these elements of communicating,
 the use of your personality style reveals your communica-
 tion style. Importantly, learning to flex your personality style
 enhances your communication effectiveness as you engage
 with others through each element of communication.
All communication styles can be equally effective. Also, com-
 munication styles that don’t match the needs of the audience
 or receiver of the communication can result in a failed com-
 munication. You need to understand the benefits and poten-
 tial challenges each style tends to demonstrate, inclusive of
 paraverbal and nonverbal behavior.
Communication is complex. You have to consider the mes-
 sage that your words, tone, and body language give to your
 audience. All of it is magnified by your personality style.
