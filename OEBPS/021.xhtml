Interestingly, communication involves more than the words
 you use. Some experts argue that up to 70 percent of the
 meaning people take from your efforts to communicate come
 from your paraverbal (voice tone) and nonverbal behaviors
 (facial expressions and body language). Your paraverbal
 behavior — the way you say what you want to share — is cen-
 tral to getting your message across. Your body language —
 nonverbal behavior — covers everything from how you shake
 hands to facial expressions and is part of your communication
 style.
Your communication style is the product of the mixture of your
 assertiveness and emotional expressiveness that makes your
 personality style, which is blended with all of the other aspects
 of communication. In other words, each style has general
 patterns in paraverbals and nonverbals, which are important
 to understand.
