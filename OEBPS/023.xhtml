Direct Style
The Direct Style works best when the audience or recipient
 of the message needs direction and responds well to asser-
 tive and low expressive behaviors. Individuals with this style
 tend to speak quickly and loudly, give direct eye contact, and
 display forms of power through their attire and by keeping
 physical distance.
A few benefits of this style in communication include

 tive communicator.
Spirited Style
The Spirited Style works best when the audience or recipient
 of the message enjoys spontaneity, enthusiastic brainstorm-
 ing, and emotional expressiveness. Individuals with this style
 tend to be animated, have a lot of voice inflection and display
 movement during interactions, and express views readily.
