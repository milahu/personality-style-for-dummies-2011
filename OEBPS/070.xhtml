Systematic Style
The Systematic Style learner is the most successful when he
 or she creates a detailed plan and pays attention to specific
 data. They are most effective as a learner when using an
 objective, methodical strategy.
The following benefits apply to the Systematic Style Learner:
 vr Comprehensively capturing information and making logi-
 cal connections for how to use it
vr Highly organized and logical in processing learning
vr Thinking-oriented, with strong reading and researching
 skills
