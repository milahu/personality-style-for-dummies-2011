Tactics to use to enhance your awareness of when to flex
 include
vr Ask the buyer if more or less assertiveness would benefit
 the interaction with a question such as “Would you like
 for me to cover all the features of this product now?”
vr Ask the buyer if a more or a less personal approach
 would benefit the interaction with a question such as
 “How can I help you identify how this product meets
 your needs?”
vr Monitor the time you spend “telling” versus “asking” in
 an interaction. If you haven’t heard the customer’s voice
 in the last 60 seconds, you're talking too much.
vr Evaluate the pace of moving through selling elements
 and processes.
Of course the best sellers seek and respond to feedback about
 what is working in the selling relationship. And sometimes the
 seller needs a coach to clarify what aspects of the personality
 style need flexing.
