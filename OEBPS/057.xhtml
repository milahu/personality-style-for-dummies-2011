An important determinant of the success of time manage-
 ment is whether tasks are completed correctly and in a timely
 manner. When so many demands are placed on your time, the
 sequencing of tasks also becomes essential to effective time
 management.
Because there are so many important aspects to time manage-
 ment, learning to flex your style is essential. The Systematic
 style may be great for getting it done right but may take an
 extra month to get done, while the Direct Style may meet the
 deadline but gloss over some of the details that they feel are
 unimportant. Flexing requires you to assess the needs of the
 situation and to shift (even momentarily) in your choice of a
 response.
Some of the common flexing challenges for the four time man-
 agement styles are as follows:
=” Direct Time Management Style can result in a failure to

 time to get feedback from others or holding a focus
 group can result in a better outcome for those with this
 style.
vw Spirited Time Management Style can cause you to get

 and lose sight of the finish line. Setting firm deadlines
 and sticking to them benefits those with this style.
vr Systematic Time Management Style can cause you to get
