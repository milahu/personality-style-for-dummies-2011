Learners have been broadly grouped according to tactics
they employ in taking in information and acting on it. These
 tactics are
vr Learning with others, the need to exchange information
 and ideas
vr Learning through independent strategies which rarely
 involve others
vw Learning through trial and error, taking action to test out
 = anidea
vr Learning by thinking things through, personal analysis,
 and the critique of information and options
As you engage in each of these learning tactics, the use of
 your personality style reveals your learning style. Importantly,
 flexing your personality and learning style enhances your
 learning effectiveness.
All learning styles can be equally effective but not equally
 effective at all times. Some learning styles work best in certain
 situations. Your task is to make sure that you're using the
 learning style that works best with the demands of the situa-
 tion you are facing. You need to understand the benefits and
 potential challenges each style tends to demonstrate.
