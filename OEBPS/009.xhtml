Chapter 1: Defining Personality Style 9
Checking Out the Four
 Personality Styles
Your style affects how you sell, coach, influence, manage time,
 lead, and learn; it even helps you engage in relationships and
 identify a career choice. While your style doesn’t predict your
 behavior or your choices, it gives you a handle on behavior in
 pragmatic ways and provides you with a foundation of under-
 standing for how you tend to operate.
Getting acquainted with your own style has the added benetfit
 of helping you identify the styles of those around you. By
 understanding the elements of personality style, you have a
 greater chance at building and maintaining the relationships
 and connections that matter in your life. In this section, I
 cover the different personality types, so maybe you can start
 to see which type you are. For a deeper discussion of the per-
 sonality types, see Chapter 2.
