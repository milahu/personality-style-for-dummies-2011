Considerate Style
People who display the Considerate Style interact best when
 audiences or recipients of messages want an empathetic, sup-
 portive, and encouraging interaction. Audiences who need a
 friendly relationship work well with this style. Individuals with
 this style tend to talk slowly, use soft tones, and like close
 personal contact.
When communicating, the person of the Considerate Style
 shows these pluses:
 vr Pursues collaborative relationships to build trust
vw Listens actively and provides support during all stages of
 the communication
vr Encourages team members and demonstrates caring for
 others’ needs
Do take time to heed the trouble areas of this communication
 style, though:
vr Overly focused on rapport and emotional expressive-
