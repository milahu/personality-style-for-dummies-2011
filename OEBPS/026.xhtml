The Considerate Communication Style doesn’t work well with
 audiences or recipients of messages who want a less personal,
 “all business” approach. This style tends to remain agreeable
 and avoid conflict. Audiences or recipients of messages who
 are looking for new insight or opinions find this style difficult
 during interactions.
Flexing the Four Personality
 Styles in Communication
To increase overall effectiveness, you need to learn to flex
 your communication style as the situation requires. For exam-
 ple, the following elements of communicating may be more
 efficiently achieved with different styles:
vw Establishing a connection with the audience and making
 sure that the reason for the communication is clear is
 effectively achieved with the Considerate Style
ws Creating a common understanding of the message being
 delivered and received is effectively achieved with the
 Systematic Style
vw Facilitating the exploration of the implications of the
 communication is effectively achieved with the Spirited
 Style
vr‘ Wrapping up the message and verifying common under-
 standing is effectively achieved with the Direct Style
Of course, an important determinant of effective communi-
 cating is the fit of styles with the needs of the audience or
 recipient of the message. If an individual with a Considerate
 Style attempts to work with an individual with a Direct Style
 and neither knows how to flex, the Considerate may end up
 with hurt feelings while the Direct may end up frustrated.
