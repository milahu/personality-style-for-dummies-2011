Systematic Style
The Systematic Style works best when the coachee wants
 a detailed plan, with an attention to data and research.
 Coachees who work best with this style want an objective,
 methodical coaching strategy.
The benefits of the Systematic Style Coach are
vr Pays extreme attention to planning, process, and detail
 vr Analytical and logical thinking
 vr Presents a patient, reserved demeanor
