Flexing Vour Style Takes
 Effort and Practice
In flexing your style, you need to adjust your behavior to
 achieve a desired result. If you don’t flex, you're likely to expe-
 rience frustration and disappointment in many situations. The
 folks around you may feel the same way as you.
While all four styles are unique, there are compatibilities
 between pairs of styles. Direct and Spirited people share the
 same preference for assertive behaviors, and Spirited and
 Considerate people share the same preference for expressive
 behaviors. So there’s a good foundation to work on because
 you only need to flex your expressive or assertive behaviors,
 but not both.
You need to work a bit harder to build a better connection
 with someone if you both share the style pairings of Direct/
 Considerate or Spirited/Systematic because you both need to
 flex your expressive and assertive behaviors, and this action
 requires more effort. It may be uncomfortable at first, kind of
 like writing with your opposite hand, but it gets easier with
 practice.
The Strengths of Each Style
 Can Be Carried to Extremes
Each personality style needs to learn how to limit the strengths
 of her style in order to make the style not become abusive.
 Extremes can be trouble spots and can become a problem.
