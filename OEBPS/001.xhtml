Introduction
P ersonality style is the essence of who you are and how
 you approach life. Knowing your personality style pro-
 vides useful insight into how you communicate, lead others,
 work in teams, and a host of other interactions. Your person-
 ality style is the foundation for what’s commonly referred to
 as “people skills.”
Personality styles are based on the blending of your assertive
 and expressive behavior. These behaviors occur in patterns
 over time and across situations. Use these insights to increase
 your personal effectiveness and enrich working with others.
About This Book
Personality Style For Dummies, HRDQ Special Edition, provides
 an overview of the four personality styles and then examines
 how these styles affect how you behave in a variety of work-
 place situations. Use this book as a guide to learn about the
 four personality styles and how these styles work in coaching,
 leading, communicating, selling, teaming, time management,
 and learning.
HRDQ has devoted considerable research and resources to
 this topic, and it is this body of work that provides the foun-
 dation for the chapters in this book. Throughout, I reference
 the HRDQ Style Model to introduce and illustrate the charac-
 teristics, benefits, and potential trouble spots associated with
 each style.
