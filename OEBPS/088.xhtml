Choose Work That Suits Vour
 Personality Style
WNG/
If you find yourself having to flex your personality style too
 much or too often, you may be doing the “wrong” kind of work
 or be in a culture or environment that doesn’t suit you.
Look to your personality style for guidance as to the kind of
 work or environments in which you're likely to thrive. If you
 have a Systematic style and you prefer logical, straightfor-
 ward assignments where you can work alone, a career that
 offers this kind of work, such as Finance, would be a wise
 choice. Considerate people are good listeners and value
 personal relationships, and so they often choose careers in
 counseling or the caring professions.
