There are numerous team models and theories you can
 research and read about. All these models, however, have
 four basic elements that are present in team interactions and
 processes:
vr Establishing a connection among team members so
 everyone is clear as to why they're members of the team
 or involved at a particular time in the process
vw Creating a common understanding of the goals, methods,
 and plans for the team to do its work
vw Supporting each other in fulfilling the mutually agreed on
 plan
vr Monitoring progress and re-evaluating future courses of
 _ action
As you engage in each of these elements of teaming, the use
 of your personality style reveals your team member style.
 Importantly, learning to flex your personality style enhances
 your team member effectiveness as you engage with team
 members through each element of teaming. Your team behav-
 ior combined with your personality style makes up your team
 member style.
All team member styles can be equally effective but not
 equally effective at all times. Team members need to flex their
 style to work closely with others and to address the demands
 of the team situation. You need to understand the benefits
 and potential challenges of each style and consider how to
 flex your style to work effectively with others.
