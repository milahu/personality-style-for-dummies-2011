Conflict Can Be Traced to
 Differences in Personality
 Styles
People get along with some folks more easily than they do
 with others. While there can be a variety of reasons in play,
 differences in personality style can often be at the root of the
 problem.
Take a quick personality style audit of people with whom you
 haven't established a strong connection but want to. Look for
 any obvious differences in style, and then learn how to flex
 your style to adjust your approach. Chapter 2 talks about flex-
 ing specifically.
