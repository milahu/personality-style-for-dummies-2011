Identifying the trouble spots
The Spirited Style has some challenging areas, as well. Some
 of the trouble spots individuals who fall in this category need
 to manage are
vr Being perceived as overly dramatic
vr Generalizing and ignoring important details
vr Missing deadlines
vr Exaggerating and missing important information
vr Being overly enthusiastic, leading to miscommunication
 about important points
Flexing the Spirited Style
Flexing the Spirited Style requires you to rein in your asser-
 tive or expressive behaviors in some situations. Because
the Spirited Style uses high degrees of both assertive and
 expressive behaviors, it’s important to read the situations and
 people you're dealing with to see if you may need to tone it
 down a little. The Spirited Style is useful in situations where
 you need to
vr Generate enthusiasm about ideas and possibilities
vr Inspire commitment to key goals and visions
vw Strengthen alliances to keep everyone working in the
 same direction
vr Rally support for pursuing key objectives
Those with the Spirited Style should adjust how much asser-
 tive behavior is appropriate and how much emotional energy
 to exhibit, depending on their surroundings. For example,
 demonstrating a Spirited Style while making a presentation
 to introduce an exciting new product works very well, but
 this style may not work so well when the negative results of a
 health and safety audit need to be explained.
