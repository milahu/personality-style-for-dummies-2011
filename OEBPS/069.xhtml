Direct Style
Someone with the Direct Style learns best when taking control
 of her own learning and is often motivated to learn by compet-
 ing against herself and others.
The benefits of the Direct Style learner include
= » Taking charge to figure out what needs to be learned and
 how to use the information
vr Remaining action-oriented and motivated when learning
 something new
v» Thriving in a competitive environment, they quickly
 absorb information to keep their edge
Along with the benefits, certain potentials for trouble exist
 and are displayed in the following ways:
= v» Appearing impatient with help from others and ignoring
 their own feelings about what is happening during the
 learning
vr Seeming overly competitive about applying new learning,
 resulting in incomplete application or premature applica-
 tion of information
| vw Acting too quickly and not providing adequate reflection
 on the information
The Direct Style doesn’t work well when a deep understand-
 ing is necessary to fully learn new information.
Spirited Style
The Spirited Style learns best when she’s energetically
 engaged with others, enjoying spontaneous brainstorming,
 and emotional linkages to the new learning.
The benefits of this style come out in the following ways:
=” Showing enthusiasm for learning, even seeking out learn-
 ing opportunities
Thriving at hands on learning, especially in groups
vw” Learning best through experimentation
