Time is nonrenewable, so how you use it is important. Your
 personality style can help you become efficient, but it can
 also create barriers to your success. The four styles approach
 the use of time differently. In this section, you find out how to
 use the four personality styles to leverage your strengths and
 minimize the behaviors that have the potential to cause you
 problems.
Direct Style
The Direct Style works best when time is of the essence.
 Those with this style show a singular drive toward a goal,
 which moves tasks along quickly.
