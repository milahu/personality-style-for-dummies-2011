Flexing requires you to assess the needs of the interaction
 and to shift (even momentarily) in your choice of a response.
 You need to determine how much assertiveness and emo-
 tional expressiveness is required of you in an interaction.
In addition, you will need to modify your paraverbal and
 nonverbal behaviors associated with the four communication
 styles.
Some of the common flexing challenges for the four communi-
 cation styles are as follows:
= vw” Direct Style communication can fail to recognize that
the individual being communicated with has hurt feel-
 ings due to a direct comment. Knowing when to increase
 some emotional expressiveness and tone down assertive-
 ness benefits those with this communication style. Also,
 making sure to speak less loudly, move less quickly, and
 move closer to others promotes the effectiveness of this
 style.
vw Spirited Style communication can fail to recognize when
 the audience wants a concrete plan and wants to know
 the details about a message. Knowing when to moderate
 both assertiveness (around ideas) and emotional expres-
 siveness helps those with this coaching style. Also, make
 sure to moderate the animated loud voice, tone down the
 inflection, and show greater calm when communicating
 with those whose styles are obviously different.
vr Systematic Style communication can fail to recognize
 that an audience or recipient of a message wants more
 brainstorming of possibilities and greater enthusiasm
 about the message. Knowing when to elevate both asser-
 tiveness and emotional expressiveness benefits those
 with this communication style. Also, make sure to share
 emotional reactions to information, engage with more
 animation, and move closer to the audience.
vr Considerate Style communication can fail to recognize
 that an audience or recipient of the message wants more
 directness and more attention to a thoroughly planned
 message than a warm, empathetic interaction. Knowing
 when to elevate an assertive response and decrease emo-
 tional expressiveness will benefit those with this com-
 munication style. Also, make sure to enhance your voice
 volume, move more quickly when physically shifting, and
 be mindful of others’ personal space.
