Considerate Style
Those with the Considerate Style work best with team mem-
 bers who want an empathetic, supportive, and encouraging
 colleague. Team members who need a friendly relationship
 will work well with this style. This style says, “I'm interested
 in your perspective. What do you think?”
The benefits of the Considerate Style as a team member include
 = Building a trusting, collaborative relationship with team
 members
vw Listening actively and mediating conflict within the team
» Remaining flexible and being comfortable in taking on
 less high-profile tasks
As with all styles, there is potential for trouble. Those with
 the Considerate Style should beware of potential blockers to
 their success:
=” This person becomes so focused on harmony that they can
 appear passive or dependent to other team members.
Wr The desire to help others creates an imbalance within
 team tasks and a feeling of being overwhelmed.
vw With building relationships being the key focus, impor-
 tant tasks or details may be overlooked.
