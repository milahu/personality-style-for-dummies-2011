Comprehending the
 Considerate Style
Do you have the desire to know what everyone else is think-
 ing, and do you seek to build relationships by being friendly
 and accommodating? If so, you've landed yourself in the
 Considerate Style. This style demonstrates a high emotional
 expressiveness and very little assertiveness. If you display
 this style, you typically are thoughtful, trusting, caring, and
 supportive of others.
The benefits of the Considerate Style are numerous.
 Individuals who prefer this style often demonstrate a keen
 interest in working with others and building relationships.
 Individuals with this style like to work with others to get
 things done.
