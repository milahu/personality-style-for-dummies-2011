Sifting through the
 Systematic Style
Careful planning and thorough organization in your approach
 to getting things done are the key elements of the Systematic
 Style. This style pays close attention to details, efficiency,
 and careful problem-solving. Generally, individuals with this
 style seem very focused on priorities and make sure to follow
 guidelines or standard operating procedures.
The Systematic Style is based on being more focused on inter-
 nal awareness than externally asserting ideas or information
 and controlling emotions. The Systematic Style is the result of
 low assertive and low emotionally expressive behaviors. For
 more on where this style lands on the style continuum, see
 Chapter 1.
The Systematic Style has the following characteristics:
Wr Follows the guidelines and standard procedures
 vr Gets results by working efficiently
 vr Organizes details, steps, and processes
vr Makes decisions using verified facts and reliable methods
vr Pursues high standards through careful analysis
Spotting the trouble areas
The Systematic Style brings about certain challenges. Often
 those who prefer the Systematic Style are unaware that their
 behavior triggers reactions that can create problems.
Some of the trouble spots include the following:
vr Being perceived as impersonal and cold
 vr Appearing disinterested in interacting with others
vr Seeming overly reliant on tried-and-true procedures at
 the cost of new ideas and innovation
vw Failing to build collaborative relationships
